Scapsense

Deeply motivated by world-building from narrative-driven IPs such as those by Hayao Miyazaki, Joe Hisaishi, and Arcane, my approach focuses on cultural relevance and emotional resonance.

A big part of how I think about design comes from paying close attention to things that genuinely move me. Joe's score for The Boy and the Heron is minimalistic, almost restrained, yet each note fluidly carries the weight of the whole film. The way Miyazaki builds his worlds is such that the most minor character feels fully placed, not decorative. How Arcane makes every frame and every soundtrack feel inseparable. For me, that's what relevance and resonance actually look like.

While being open, I’m particularly drawn to opportunities involving music, movies, art, culture, entertainment, tech, media production, streaming, soundtracking, and recording.
